# News

Articles about ChromaDotNet, written by the author of the project.

  * [The Aspire integration of ChromaDotNet joins the Aspire Community Toolkit](https://chromadotnet.org/news/aspire-community-toolkit/)

Federico Laurianti, 25 October 2026

The Chroma hosting and client integrations of ChromaDotNet are merged into the Aspire Community Toolkit. Since 4 November 2026 they are on NuGet in beta.

  * [The Chroma provider for Microsoft.Extensions.VectorData joins the AI Community Toolkit](https://chromadotnet.org/news/chroma-ai-community-toolkit/)

Federico Laurianti, 13 October 2026

The Chroma provider for Microsoft.Extensions.VectorData is now in the AI Community Toolkit of the .NET Foundation, as CommunityToolkit.VectorData.Chroma. It is built on ChromaDotNet.Client.

  * [ChromaDotNet: open source .NET libraries for Chroma and Chroma Cloud](https://chromadotnet.org/news/chromadotnet/)

Federico Laurianti, 5 October 2026

A client for the whole Chroma v2 API, Chroma without Docker, the default embedding function, a Testcontainers module, Aspire integrations and a Kubernetes operator. On NuGet, MIT licensed.
